Hatzolah Archive Uploader

Hatzolah Archive Uploader is a personal, single-user tool run by its owner. It uploads audio archive files and their metadata from the owner's own scanner-feed collection to the owner's own Google Drive, using the Google Drive API.

It has no other users and no public sign-up. It does not collect, store, or share anyone else's data.
