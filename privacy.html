Privacy Policy

Hatzolah Archive Uploader is a personal OAuth app used only by its owner.

What it accesses: the owner's own Google Drive files, via the Google Drive API, solely to create folders and upload the owner's own archive files.

What it stores: nothing outside the owner's own Google Drive and the owner's own credential storage.

What it shares: nothing. No data is sold, transferred, or disclosed to any third party. Google user data is used only to provide this single-owner upload function and for no other purpose.
