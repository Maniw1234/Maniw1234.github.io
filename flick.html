Phone to PC, with a flick.

Flick sends photos, videos and any file between your Android phone and your Windows, Mac or Linux computer. Throw a file off the edge of the phone's screen and it lands on the PC. No cable, no account, no cloud.

What Flick does

An AirDrop-style way to move things between Android and a computer, and a few things more.

Send any file, both ways

Photos, videos, documents, whole folders. Phone to PC, PC to phone, phone to phone and PC to PC.

Fast

Files go directly over your Wi-Fi, a USB cable, a hotspot or Bluetooth. Large files use several connections, and a send that is cut off carries on where it stopped.

Private

No account, no ads, no analytics, no cloud. Transfers are encrypted between your own devices. Devices pair once, with a code you check on both screens.

Clipboard and links

Copy on one device and paste on the other, or send a link straight to the phone's browser.

Notifications on your PC

See your phone's notifications pop up on the computer while you work.

Synced folders

Keep a folder on the phone and a folder on the PC the same, in both directions.

Media remote and Find my phone

Play, pause and skip on the PC from the phone, and ring the phone from the PC.

A file manager too

Every file on the phone sorted into Photos, Videos, Downloads, Documents and more, with search and previews.

How it works

  1. Install both appsThe computer app for Windows, Mac or Linux, and the Android app on your phone.
  2. Hold ConnectHold the Connect button on either one, allow it on the other, and check that both show the same code.
  3. FlickDrag a file off the edge of the phone's screen toward your PC. It lands there.

Questions

How do I send files from my Android phone to my PC without a cable?

Install Flick on the phone and on the computer, hold Connect on one of them and allow it on the other. Then flick a file off the edge of the phone's screen, toward the computer, and it is saved there. Files travel over your own Wi-Fi, a hotspot or Bluetooth.

Is Flick free?

Yes. Flick is free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads, no account and no paid tier.

Does Flick upload my files to the cloud?

No. Files go straight from one of your devices to the other, encrypted, and never pass through a server on the internet.

Which devices does Flick work on?

Android 9 or newer on the phone, and Windows, macOS 13 or newer, or Linux on the computer. It also sends from phone to phone and from computer to computer.

Can I use Flick with an iPhone?

There is no iPhone app, but the computer app has Share with a browser: an iPhone, iPad or any other device on the same network can send and receive files through its web browser.
